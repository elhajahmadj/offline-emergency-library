- **Space estimate now prints correct totals under a decimal-comma locale.** The Bash installer's estimate formatted and re-parsed its GB figures with `awk`, so on an `awk` that honors `LC_NUMERIC` (mawk, macOS/BSD `awk`) a locale like `de_DE.UTF-8` rendered `15.4` as `15,4`, and the running total then read it as `15` — understating the budget and skipping the "not enough space" warning. Every float `awk` in the estimate (and the guided-install preset fit) is now pinned to `LC_ALL=C`, so the maths and the warning are locale-independent, matching the PowerShell side (which already formats with the invariant culture).

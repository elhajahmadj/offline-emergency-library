Medical — Offline Health & First-Aid Reference

Offline medical reference for emergencies. Two kinds of files:

  • PDFs → open in Edge or any web browser. No software needed.
  • .zim archives → open with Kiwix (portable, in E:\Kiwix-Reader\). See

E:\Kiwix-Reader\HOW-TO-USE-KIWIX.

What's here

Folder Read with Contents
WikiMed\ Kiwix (.zim) Offline medical Wikipedia (with images) — ~50,000 health articles
zimgit-medicine\ Kiwix (.zim) Curated offline medical / first-aid guide
Manuals-PDF\ Browser (PDF) *Where There Is No Doctor*, *Where There Is No Dentist* (Hesperian); A Practical Guide to Prolonged Casualty Care (Uniformed Services University + Henry M. Jackson Foundation, 2025; © 2025 Henry M. Jackson Foundation, CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, included unchanged). Advanced reference for when medical help is delayed for hours or days — not a substitute for a clinician.
PSYCHOLOGICAL-FIRST-AID.html Browser (html) Psychological first aid — plain-language guide to helping people (and yourself) cope with fear, panic, grief and stress during a crisis. Mental health matters as much as physical injury.

Also relevant: the Army first-aid manuals in E:\Manuals\ArmyFieldManuals\.

Ask an AI about the PDFs

Use GPT4All (E:\AI-Offline\DocReader-GPT4All\, see HOW-TO-ASK-YOUR-DOCS) and add

E:\Medical\Manuals-PDF as a collection, then ask things like *"how do I treat a burn?"*

It answers from the books and cites the source. Browse WikiMed in Kiwix.

⚠️ Important

This is reference information, not a substitute for a doctor. Models and books can be

wrong or out of date. In a real emergency, get professional medical help whenever

possible. Use this when no help is available, and double-check anything critical.

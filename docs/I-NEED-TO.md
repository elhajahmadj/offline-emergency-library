# 🆘 I need to… — emergency quick index

**If professional help is reachable, get it first.** Call your local emergency number
(911 in the US), or use the [radio guide](Manuals/Radio-Communications/RADIO-GUIDE.html)
to call for help. Use this drive when no professional help is available.

This page sends you **straight to the right file**. It is not the instructions — it points
to the manual that has them. The manuals can be wrong or dated; follow the most trustworthy
source you have, and use your own judgement.

> **How to open what's linked here:** **PDFs** and **.html** guides open in **any web
> browser** (just click). **`.zim`** archives open in **Kiwix** — open the Kiwix reader,
> then search inside the archive (see [how to use Kiwix](Kiwix-Reader/HOW-TO-USE-KIWIX.html)).
> If a link doesn't open, that part wasn't included in your build.

> 🖨️ **No power?** This page needs a screen. Print **[Emergency essentials](EMERGENCY-ESSENTIALS.html)**
> now — one page of the most critical steps that works on paper when the battery is dead.

**More depth:** `Medical\WikiMed\` (medical Wikipedia, open in **Kiwix**) and
`Medical\Manuals-PDF\` (first-aid PDFs: Where There Is No Doctor / No Dentist). See the
[medical read-me](Medical/READ-ME-FIRST.html).

**Historical herbals (not medical advice):** `Medical\Natural-Medicine\` holds *King's American
Dispensatory* and Culpeper's *Complete Herbal* as **historical references** on medicinal plants.
They are old (1600s–1900s), often inaccurate, and some remedies are unsafe. Use current sources
(WikiMed, Where There Is No Doctor) for actual care — see the [medical read-me](Medical/READ-ME-FIRST.html).

**What is this illness, symptom or test?** If you added it (`-MedlinePlus` /
`--medlineplus`), [MedlinePlus health topics](Medical/MedlinePlus/MedlinePlus-Health-Topics.html)
explains health problems and tests in plain language (English and Spanish), in any browser.

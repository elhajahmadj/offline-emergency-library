Sine Nexu hosts nothing itself. Every file the installer downloads comes from an official upstream source, grouped below by what it is, with its license. After the one-time download the whole library runs with no internet. Pieces tagged opt-in are only fetched when you choose them.

This is the plain-language version of the repository's DATA-SOURCES.md, which lists the exact URLs. CI re-checks every source weekly via check-links.sh.

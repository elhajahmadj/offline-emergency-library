Sine Nexu is a free, open-source project with a simple privacy stance: we don't want your data, and we don't collect it. This policy covers the Sine Nexu website (the landing page, the picker, and these pages). It is written in plain language; there is no legal small print because there is almost nothing to disclose.

Last updated: November 2026.

What we collect

Nothing about you. This website has no accounts, no sign-in, no contact forms, no cookies, no analytics, and no third-party trackers or advertising. We do not build profiles, and we have no way to identify you.

The picker stays on your device

When you use the picker to choose what goes on your drive, every choice is handled entirely in your browser. The selection.json file it produces is generated locally and saved to your computer — it is never uploaded or sent to us or to anyone else. You can use the picker with no internet at all by opening it from a local copy.

Hosting & server logs

The website is served as static files by our hosting provider (Fly.io). Like any web server, it may automatically keep short-lived technical logs of requests — typically an IP address, the time, and the page or file requested — purely to run the service, keep it secure, and diagnose problems. We don't use these logs to track or identify visitors, and there is no analytics layer on top of them. Fly.io processes this data as our hosting provider under its own privacy terms.

Downloads come straight from the original sources

The installer downloads files directly from the upstream providers listed on the Sources page (such as the Internet Archive, Kiwix, OpenStreetMap/OsmAnd, Hugging Face, GitHub, WHO and others) — not through us. When you download from those sites, their own privacy policies apply to that connection. We never see what you download or where you are.

The library runs offline

Once downloaded, the Sine Nexu library is designed to run with no internet. The guides, maps, archives and the local AI assistant work entirely on your own device and do not phone home to us or anyone else.

Children

The site is a general-audience reference resource and does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes & contact

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page. Questions or concerns? Open an issue or discussion on the project on GitHub.

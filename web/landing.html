Knowledge that doesn't need the internet.

Sine Nexu — a complete offline emergency library you download with one command.

One command downloads a complete offline, no-internet library: a local AI assistant (CPU or GPU), document Q&A, full Wikipedia, offline maps for any country or region, and survival, repair and medical archives plus public-domain manuals. It asks only where to save — then everything works with no internet and no power grid behind it.

The name is Latin: sine nexu, “without a connection”. That's the point.

Why we built it

The internet is where almost all practical knowledge lives — until the moment you can't reach it. A storm takes out the power. A disaster takes out the network. You're somewhere remote with no signal at all. That's exactly when you most need to know how to stay safe, treat an injury, purify water, grow or preserve food, fix a critical piece of equipment, or find your way.

Sine Nexu exists for those moments. It's built for emergencies and grid-down situations, and for people in remote parts of the world who have little or no internet access. The whole library lives on a drive you own, so a phone or a laptop and that drive can be the reference you fall back on when there's nothing else.

What's inside

Offline AI assistant

A local chat model (CPU or GPU) and document Q&A you can ask in plain language — no account, no cloud.

All of Wikipedia

The full encyclopedia as an offline Kiwix archive, plus optional Wikivoyage and Wikibooks.

Offline maps

OsmAnd maps for any country or region (US by default), viewable on the desktop and in a browser.

Survival & repair

Survival guides, the iFixit repair archive and the Appropedia DIY archive for fixing and making things.

Medical references

Plain-language health topics and medical guidance for when help isn't a phone call away.

Public-domain manuals

Army field manuals and verified public-domain books on food-growing, metalworking, fishing and more.

How it works

  1. Pick what you want. Open the picker, tick the pieces and map regions you need, and it saves a tiny selection.json. Or just choose “install everything”.
  2. Run one command. Double-click the installer for your system (install.bat, install.command or install.sh). It asks only where to save.
  3. Use it anywhere, offline. It writes every guide in Markdown and HTML, opens from START-HERE.html, and keeps working with no internet — for years.

You need a drive with room to spare (an external SSD is ideal). You choose how much to download — from a lean kit to the full library.
